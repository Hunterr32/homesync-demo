HomeSync

A shared household chore tracker for iPhone. This is an interactive preview of how the app looks and works.

About this demo

  • Not connected to Firebase or any account, server or database. It runs entirely in your browser.
  • Nothing is saved. Changes disappear when you refresh or close the page.
  • Everyone in it (Riley, Alex, Sam and Taylor) and every home is made up.

Try it: open Maple Street House, tick off a chore and watch the room's status change, add a note to a room, or tap Organize my day.
